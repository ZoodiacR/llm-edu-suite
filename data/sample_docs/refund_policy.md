# Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are issued as store credit only.
Annual subscriptions cancelled in the first 14 days are refunded in full; after that, the unused months are refunded pro rata.
Refunds are processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method.
Digital gift cards are non-refundable.
